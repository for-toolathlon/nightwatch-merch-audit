# Merch Audit: The Night Watch

## Artwork record

- **Artist:** Rembrandt held to Rembrandt van Rijn (identified as Rembrandt's)
- **Object number:** SK-C-5
- **Official title:** *Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht'* (The Militia Company of Captain Frans Banninck Cocq and Lieutenant Willem van Ruytenburch, known as the 'Night Watch')
- **Dating:** 1642
- **Technique/materials:** The collection record attributes oil paint on canvas to the work (oil on canvas — the Rijksmuseum's collection record attributes oil paint on canvas to the work)
- **Career placement:** One of Rembrandt's masterpiece late-period group portraits, painted after his 1630s Brueghel-esque grand-scale portraits; a milestone showing his large-scale mythology and civic history themes and his rich handling of light, alongside his Night Watch and late biblical history paintings.

## Market side highlights

Amazon carries a range of merchandise connected to the painting — prints/posters, puzzle/box sets (e.g. Ao Peleglioli / several retail sellers), and canvas reproductions / framed prints — but volumes and fidelity vary.
