## 1. Artwork Record (Rijksmuseum collection)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Artist** | Rembrandt van Rijn |
| **Object number** | SK-C-5 |
| **Official title (Dutch)** | Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de *Nachtwacht* |
| **English title** | The Police Company of Captain Frans Banninck Cocq and Lieutenant Willem van Ruytenburch, known as the Night Watch |
| **Date** | 1642 |
| **Current location** | Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam |
| **Art period** | Golden Age / Dutch Republic, faction portrait (often classified as a *maatschappelijk portret*) |

**Technique / materials (from collection record):** Oil on *verre colour* (glazed oil paint) on canvas.

**One-line career note:** The *Nachtwacht* stands as the climax of Rembrandt's Amsterdam milestone work — his mythic, large-scale (4.35 × 3.64 m) civic-portrait cycle for the newly integrated ranks: It is his career's equivalent of something El Greco would later earn from the Habsburgs, the high-water mark of his "Figure-period" maturity (c. 1625–1629) that followed the Leiden Reformation of 1626. After it he turned to *portraits inside rooms and portraits of married couples* (a markedly different genre from hiding figures out in landscape as here).
